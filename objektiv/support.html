Getting started

Allow camera access or import a photo. Choose a camera look, adjust frame, date and intensity, then use the shutter. Movie looks enable video recording. Open the film roll to review and share your images.

Permissions

Camera access enables capture. Microphone access enables relevant sound and video features. Saving to Photos requires add access. Ghost photos use photo-library and location permissions to find older photos taken nearby. Change permissions in iOS Settings.

Custom cameras and captions

Apple's on-device language model is used when available on your device. An offline interpretation and simple caption fallback remain available without the model. Generated results can vary.

Nearby sharing

Keep compatible devices nearby, with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and local-network access available where needed. Leave the participating apps open. Delivery and relay are not guaranteed. Public sharing is visible to nearby recipients; choose private transfer for a specific contact and verify their key fingerprint through a trusted channel.

Storage

Your roll and custom cameras are stored locally. Export images you want to keep before removing the app. Nearby recipients retain their own copies of shared items.
