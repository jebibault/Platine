Support

Contact Jean-Emmanuel Bibault at jebibault@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send private files or secret keys.

Start nearby

Choose a nickname, open Private sending and activate a session. Keep compatible devices close with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled. Allow local-network access when requested. Exchange contacts before sending private items. Compare key fingerprints through a trusted channel; nicknames are not unique and do not verify a person's identity.

Relays and delivery

Private envelopes may be carried by nearby devices for up to 24 hours. Delivery depends on encounters and is not guaranteed. Keep participating apps open. The app does not provide always-on background discovery or an emergency communications service.

Public files

Open Public broadcasts to publish up to five prepared files or discover nearby sources. These files are public, not confidential. You can block a source and stop a broadcast; already downloaded copies remain with recipients. Public storage is limited to 20 files / 5 MB. Files do not open automatically.

Local live video

Choose a nickname, then broadcast or discover a nearby live stream. Camera and microphone permissions are required for capture. Local Wi-Fi supports up to three viewers, 640-pixel video, up to 10 fps and mono audio. Streams are not relayed and the app does not record them. A shared Wi-Fi network also works without Internet access.

Sharing from other apps

Choose meshdrop from the iOS share sheet, then open meshdrop to use the prepared draft. Media is compressed for transport. Video becomes a short silent clip; originals are retained.

Permissions and deletion

Manage Bluetooth, local network, camera, microphone and optional notification permissions in iOS Settings. Remove received content in the app. Export files you want to keep before uninstalling.

Assistance

Écrivez à jebibault@gmail.com avec votre modèle d’iPhone, vos versions d’iOS et de meshdrop et les étapes du problème. Choisissez un pseudo, activez une session privée et gardez les deux apps ouvertes, Bluetooth et Wi-Fi activés. Comparez les empreintes de clés avec votre contact. Les relais conservent les enveloppes chiffrées au maximum 24 heures ; la livraison n’est jamais garantie.

Les diffusions publiques sont visibles des destinataires proches, qui peuvent garder leurs copies. Le direct utilise le Wi-Fi local, avec trois spectateurs maximum, et ne passe pas par les relais. Les permissions se règlent dans Réglages iOS. La feuille de partage prépare des copies compressées en conservant les originaux. meshdrop n’est pas un service de communication d’urgence.
